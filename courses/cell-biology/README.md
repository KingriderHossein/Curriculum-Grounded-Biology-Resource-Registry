## Course boundary

This page covers the **theoretical course only**. The laboratory companion course and all procedural training are excluded.

The curriculum describes Cell Biology as a 3-credit, 48-hour theoretical course with Structural Biochemistry as prerequisite.

## Official syllabus topics

| ID | Topic |
|---|---|
| CB01 | Chemical basis of life; origin/evolution of cells; concepts and history of cell biology |
| CB02 | Cell theory; prokaryotic/eukaryotic organization; multicellularity; organelle evolution; conceptual overview of methods used to study cells |
| CB03 | Biological membranes: structure, physicochemical properties, functions, and transmembrane transport |
| CB04 | Endomembrane organelles: rough/smooth ER, Golgi, lysosomes, peroxisomes, endosomes, vacuoles, transport vesicles |
| CB05 | Endocytosis and exocytosis; lysosomal roles in endocytosis, phagocytosis, and autophagy |
| CB06 | Mitochondria: origin, structure, function, protein import, mitochondrial genome, inter-organelle relationships |
| CB07 | Plastids and chloroplasts: origin, types, structure, function, protein import, chloroplast genome |
| CB08 | Nucleus and nucleolus; nuclear structure/function; protein and RNA exchange between cytosol and nucleus |
| CB09 | Eukaryotic cell division: mitosis, meiosis, cytokinesis, cell-cycle overview and regulation |
| CB10 | Cytoskeleton: microtubules, actin filaments, intermediate filaments; organization and function |
| CB11 | Extracellular matrix, cell adhesion molecules, cell–matrix and cell–cell junctions |
| CB12 | Plant cell wall: structure and function |

The source also mentions methods for studying cells. In this registry they are covered only as **theoretical concepts**, not as practical skills or protocols.

## Current-update reviews

These do not replace the textbooks. They update topics where the field has moved quickly.

### Organelle communication

**Calì T, Bayer EM, Eden ER, et al. (2025). _Key challenges and recommendations for defining organelle membrane contact sites_. Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology 26:776–796.**

- DOI: [10.1038/s41580-025-00864-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41580-025-00864-x)
- Adds a current view of membrane contact sites, organelle communication, and exchange of ions/lipids/metabolites.
- Mapped topics: **CB04, CB06, CB07**

[Journal page](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41580-025-00864-x)

### Nuclear pore architecture and transport

**Petrovic S, Mobbs GW, Hoelz A. (2025; issue 2026). _Structure, function and assembly of nuclear pore complexes_. Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology 27:35–54.**

- DOI: [10.1038/s41580-025-00881-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41580-025-00881-w)
- Synthesizes recent near-atomic NPC structures, selective permeability, nucleocytoplasmic transport, and NPC assembly.
- Mapped topic: **CB08**

[Journal page](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41580-025-00881-w)
